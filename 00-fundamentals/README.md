# 00 — AI Fundamentals

## 🎯 Objective

Build a solid foundation in the core mathematical and theoretical concepts underlying modern AI systems. By the end of this module, you should understand:

- How neural networks learn through gradient descent and backpropagation
- The role of probability and statistics in machine learning
- Key concepts in deep learning (layers, activation functions, loss functions)
- Why these foundations matter for working with LLMs and agents

---

## 🧠 Concepts

- **Linear Algebra**: Vectors, matrices, and why they matter in ML
- **Calculus & Gradients**: How neural networks optimize through differentiation
- **Probability & Statistics**: Distributions, inference, Bayesian thinking
- **Neural Networks Basics**: Layers, activation functions, forward/backward pass
- **Loss Functions & Optimization**: Understanding training dynamics
- **Deep Learning Intuition**: Why deep networks work, common patterns

## 💻 Practical Work

- Implement a simple neural network from scratch (NumPy)
- Train on MNIST or similar simple dataset
- Experiment with different learning rates and observe convergence
- Understand backpropagation through visualization

---

## ✅ Validation

You'll know you've mastered this module when you can:

- Explain why neural networks use calculus and gradients
- Implement gradient descent by hand for a simple problem
- Understand the relationship between loss functions and model behavior
- Recognize different activation functions and when to use them
- Have intuition for why deep networks can learn complex patterns
