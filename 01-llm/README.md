# 01 — LLM Fundamentals

## 🎯 Objective

Understand how Large Language Models work from first principles. By the end of this module, you should:

- Understand the Transformer architecture and why it revolutionized NLP
- Know how tokenization works and why it matters
- Understand embeddings and the relationship between tokens and meaning
- Grasp the difference between pretraining, fine-tuning, and inference
- Recognize the capabilities and limitations of modern LLMs

---

## 🧠 Concepts

- **Transformer Architecture**: Attention mechanism, multi-head attention, self-attention
- **Tokenization**: How text is converted to tokens, special tokens, vocabulary
- **Embeddings**: Learned representations of tokens, semantic meaning
- **Attention Mechanism**: How transformers "pay attention" to different parts of text
- **Pretraining vs Fine-tuning**: Different training approaches and their tradeoffs
- **Scaling Laws**: Why larger models are different, emergent abilities
- **Inference**: How models generate text token-by-token (autoregressive generation)

## 💻 Practical Work

- Experiment with tokenization using HuggingFace tokenizers
- Load and use a pre-trained LLM (e.g., with transformers library)
- Understand embedding vectors and their properties
- Experiment with inference parameters (temperature, top-k, etc.)
- Observe how model size affects capability
